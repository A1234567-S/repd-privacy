# Privacy Policy for Repd - The Trainer's Logbook

Last updated: November 3, 2026

## Your client and workout data stays on your device

Client names, sessions, exercises, notes and progress are stored locally on your iPhone. Repd has no accounts and no server of its own, and this data is never uploaded, shared, or sold.

## Anonymous usage analytics

Repd uses PostHog to understand how the app is used so it can be improved — for example, which features are opened and whether the subscription screen loaded correctly. These events are tied to a random identifier generated on your device. They contain no client data, no names, no notes, no workout content and no contact details, and the identifier is not linked to your identity.

## Advertising (free plan only)

On the free plan, Repd shows banner ads supplied by Google AdMob. To serve them, Google receives device and usage signals such as a device advertising identifier, approximate location derived from your IP address, and basic device information. Google may combine this with data it holds from other apps and websites to choose which ads you see.

Your client names, sessions, exercises, notes and progress are never shared with Google or any advertiser — that data never leaves your device at all.

iOS asks for your permission before any advertising identifier can be used. If you decline, ads still appear but are not personalised. You can change this at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Google's own privacy policy covers what it does with this data: <https://policies.google.com/privacy>. How Google uses data from apps that use its services: <https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites>.

## Repd Pro has no ads

Subscribing, or buying the one-time Lifetime option, removes advertising entirely. On a Pro account the Google advertising SDK is never started and no ad request is ever made from your device.

## Purchases

Subscriptions and the one-time Lifetime purchase are processed by Apple. RevenueCat is used to manage purchase status. Neither provides Repd with your payment details.

## Photos

If you save a session summary image, it is written to your photo library only after you grant permission. Repd can only add images — it can never read your photo library.
